Contact FAQ
How quickly do you respond to emails? +

Typically within 2–3 working days. If your message is time-sensitive, please include “URGENT” in the subject line and we will prioritise it.

Can I submit a guest article or pitch? +

Absolutely. We welcome pitches from designers, architects, and writers. Please send a short abstract (200 words) and a link to your portfolio or previous work. We review every pitch, though we cannot respond to all of them individually.

Do you accept advertising or sponsored posts? +

We selectively partner with brands that align with our editorial values. All sponsored content is clearly labelled. For media kits and rates, please contact partnerships@hardlinehomeconcepts.com.

I have a correction or update for an article. +

We take accuracy seriously. Please email editorial@hardlinehomeconcepts.com with the article URL and the specific correction. We will review and update promptly.

About

Hardline Home Concepts is an independent editorial publication dedicated to interior design and architecture. We publish in-depth articles, practical guides, and critical perspectives for designers, architects, and anyone who cares about the spaces they inhabit.

Our content is researched, written, and edited by a small team of design professionals. We are not affiliated with any product manufacturer or design firm.
