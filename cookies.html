Cookies policy

We keep things simple. This site uses only the essential cookies needed to function. Here’s exactly what that means.

Last updated: 15 January 2025

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the site owners.

This Cookies Policy explains how Hardline Home Concepts (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on hardlinehomeconcepts.com. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

In short: We use only strictly necessary cookies. No tracking. No advertising. No third-party analytics. You can browse with confidence.

2. Our approach to cookies

We believe in minimal data collection. Unlike many websites, we do not use cookies for advertising, remarketing, or behavioural tracking. We do not embed third-party analytics scripts (such as Google Analytics) that would set tracking cookies on your device.

The only cookies we use are those that are strictly necessary for the website to function correctly and securely.

3. Types of cookies we use

Below is a detailed list of the cookies that may be set when you visit our site:

Cookie name Type Purpose Duration
session_id Essential Maintains your session state across page requests. Required for basic site functionality. Session
csrf_token Essential Protects against cross-site request forgery attacks. A security measure. Session
cookie_consent Essential Stores your cookie consent preference so we don't ask again. 1 year

We do not use any performance, functional, targeting, or advertising cookies.

4. Third-party cookies

We do not allow third parties to set cookies on our site. All cookies listed above are set directly by Hardline Home Concepts.

Please note that if you click on a link to an external website (for example, a source cited in an article), that website may set its own cookies. We have no control over those cookies and recommend you check the relevant site’s cookie policy.

5. Your cookie choices

Because we only use strictly necessary cookies, we do not require your consent to set them. However, you can still control and manage cookies in your browser settings.

Most browsers allow you to:

  • View what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific sites.
  • Block all cookies.
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

Please be aware that if you block or delete essential cookies, parts of our website may not function correctly.

How to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Cookies and site data

6. Do Not Track signals

Some browsers support a “Do Not Track” (DNT) feature that signals to websites that you do not want to be tracked. Because we do not track our users across websites, we do not respond to DNT signals — there is simply no tracking to disable.

7. Changes to this Cookies Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “last updated” date. We encourage you to check back periodically.

8. Contact us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this Cookies Policy, please contact us:
